The Baker

Made by hand, one cake at a time.

Yummy Cakee is the home bakery of Olena Godyna, based in Ontario, Canada. Every cake is baked to order — no freezer stock, no shortcuts — so what arrives at your table was made for that day and that occasion.

The compositions are built around real ingredients: true vanilla, Sicilian pistachio, deep cocoa, and fresh berries in season. Decor is kept honest and elegant, from minimal bento cakes to tiered showpieces.
